# TikTok format

- Canvas 1080x1920 (9:16). Horizontal variant only when asked (suffix `-16x9`).
- Safe zones: 12 % top, 25 % bottom, 8 % sides. Keep text, logos, and buttons inside them.
- Length: tutorials 25–40 s, promos 15–25 s, tips 10–20 s. Shorter is better if the idea fits.
- The first frame is the thumbnail: the hook must be fully readable at second 0, not animated in. Animate the support text instead.
- Hook before second 2; first real content (command, screen, number) before second 5. No intro frames or index.
- Text: titles at least 80 px, body at least 46 px, max 25 words per scene, high contrast against the background (WCAG AA).
- One idea per scene. 4–8 scenes.
- Titles in normal sentence case, never ALL CAPS.
- End with a question answerable in one word (content videos) or the real CTA (promos).
- Language of on-screen text: the video's language, Spanish by default.
